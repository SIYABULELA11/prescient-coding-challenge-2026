Prescient Coding Challenge 2026

The Video

Every team records a short explanation of its solution. It is how the investment team scores your reasoning.

The ask

  • Two to five minutes. One take is fine.
  • Without a video there is no human score, and the human score is part of picking the winner.
2–5
Minutes
5
Segments, in order

What to cover

1

Who, and what you built

Team name and who is on it. Then one sentence: our strategy overweights X when Y, because Z. If you cannot say it in one sentence, say that too.

20 sec
2

The idea

Why this works on these six assets. Not that momentum works – why momentum on this benchmark, inside these bands, at these trading costs. Which assets you take a view on, which you leave alone, and why.

60–90 sec
3

The decisions

What each number in PARAMS controls, and why that value rather than double it. Where in your logic the view actually gets expressed. From memory – not read off a screen.

60–90 sec
4

What you threw away

One thing you tried that did not survive, and how you knew. A regime it failed in, a parameter that only worked on 2020.

30–60 sec
5

Where it breaks

Which of the four historical windows is your worst, and why. What you would do with another week.

20–30 sec

Two more things

Name your AI tools

Say which you used and what you used them for. It is already required in the pull request – say it out loud as well. There is no penalty. All of them are allowed.

What is not being marked

Not your English, your accent, your camera or your confidence. Read from notes if it helps. What is being marked is whether you can answer for your own decisions.

A model can write three hundred lines in ninety seconds. It cannot tell you which of three plausible signals survives a regime break. That judgement is what the scoring is built to reward, and the video is where it shows.
